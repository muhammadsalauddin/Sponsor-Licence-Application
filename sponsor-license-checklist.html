The exact supporting evidence depends on the organisation, but this checklist covers the most common employer-side preparation areas.

Before you start

  • 1 Confirm the business genuinely needs to recruit overseas workers and can explain that need clearly.
  • 2 Identify who will take responsibility for sponsor duties internally.
  • 3 Start gathering company evidence before trying to complete the application itself.
  • 4 Check whether record keeping and right to work processes are already documented or need strengthening first.
