Basic package

Eligibility and Readiness Audit

From GBP 795

For employers that want to know whether the business is ready, what is missing, and whether there is a sensible route to proceed.

Best for: first-time sponsor applicants and small businesses testing viability.

Includes: eligibility review, evidence gap analysis, role planning observations, high-level compliance findings.

Which package fits?

A simple decision guide.

Choose Basic if you first need a credible answer on eligibility, internal gaps, and whether proceeding now is sensible.

Choose Standard if the business is broadly viable but needs guided support to organise evidence and get the application ready properly.

Choose Premium if sponsor duties, internal ownership, and post-approval controls matter just as much as the application itself.
