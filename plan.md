# AI Build Brief: UK Sponsor Licence Application Website

## Project Goal
Build a high-converting static website for a consultancy that helps UK businesses secure a Sponsor Licence and hire overseas workers legally and confidently.

Primary goals:
- Generate consultation bookings from business owners and HR decision-makers
- Capture qualified employer leads
- Build trust through compliance-focused messaging

Secondary goals:
- Educate businesses about the licence process
- Explain the difference between application support and ongoing compliance support
- Make the service feel practical, serious, and commercially useful

---

## Core Positioning
This service should not feel like a generic immigration form-filling service.

Position it as:
- a compliance-led sponsor licence partner
- an application and audit-preparation specialist
- a business-focused adviser for overseas hiring readiness

Core message:
We help UK employers prepare, apply, and stay compliant when hiring international talent.

The tone should be:
- credible
- practical
- professional
- calm and compliance-aware

Avoid:
- hype-heavy promises
- shortcuts language
- anything that implies guaranteed Home Office approval

---

## Target Audience
- UK business owners
- directors and founders of SMEs
- HR managers and operations managers
- care providers, hospitality businesses, logistics firms, tech companies, professional services, and other employers planning overseas recruitment

Common audience pain points:
- uncertainty about whether the business qualifies
- confusion around Sponsor Licence documents
- lack of HR systems or compliance processes
- fear of refusal or compliance breaches
- need to hire staff urgently but correctly

---

## Recommended Deliverables
Minimum build:
- sponsor-license.html: main landing page

Recommended expansion pages if the user asks for a fuller site:
- sponsor-license-pricing.html: pricing and package comparison
- sponsor-license-checklist.html: employer document checklist
- sponsor-license-process.html: step-by-step sponsor licence roadmap
- sponsor-license-compliance.html: ongoing compliance support page

If an AI is asked to build only one page, prioritize the main landing page first.

---

## Main Messaging Rules
- Use the UK term "Sponsor Licence" in the main copy
- It is acceptable to mention "Sponsor License" once or twice for SEO if needed, but the core branding should stay UK-specific
- Speak to employers, not migrants
- Emphasize compliance, preparation, and risk reduction
- Make it clear that licence approval is subject to Home Office assessment
- Show that the consultancy helps before, during, and after application

Preferred CTA language:
- Book Sponsor Licence Consultation
- Get Compliance Review
- Check Business Eligibility
- Speak With a Sponsor Licence Expert

## Main Page Structure: sponsor-license.html

### 1. Hero Section
Purpose: immediately show what the service is and who it is for.

Suggested direction:
- headline around Sponsor Licence support for UK employers
- subheadline focused on application readiness, compliance, and overseas hiring confidence
- 2 CTA buttons above the fold

### 2. Problem Section

- confusing Home Office rules
- missing or weak documents
- poor HR compliance systems

### 3. Solution Section
Explain the service as a structured, compliance-led process.

Core service themes:
- eligibility review
- document preparation
- role and recruitment planning
- application support
- HR and compliance setup
- post-licence support

---

### 4. Services Breakdown
Use cards or a clean grid.

Suggested services:
- Eligibility & Readiness Review
- Sponsor Licence Document Pack Support
- Genuine Vacancy and Role Guidance
- Key Personnel and SMS Setup Guidance
- Application Drafting and Submission Support
- Mock Audit / Compliance Review
- Post-Licence Compliance Support

Each card should contain:

- practical business value

### 5. Who This Service Is For
Add a section that makes qualification clearer.

Suggested audiences:
- care providers
- hospitality groups
- restaurants and takeaways with structured hiring needs
- logistics and warehouse employers
- construction and trades businesses

- professional services firms

This section should help visitors self-identify quickly.

---

### 6. Why Choose Us
Possible trust angles:
- compliance-first approach
- business-friendly guidance
- practical document preparation
- support beyond submission
- clear explanation without jargon

Make this section feel serious and reassuring.

---

### 7. Sponsor Licence Process Timeline
Recommended stages:
1. Discovery call
2. Eligibility and compliance review
3. Document collection
4. Application preparation
5. Submission
6. Post-submission support
7. Post-approval compliance setup

Each stage should explain what the business needs to do and what support is provided.

---

### 8. Pricing Section
Recommended 3-tier model:
- Basic: eligibility and readiness audit
- Standard: full application preparation support
- Premium: end-to-end support plus compliance setup / ongoing guidance

Each tier should include:
- who it is for
- what is included
- what outcome it supports
- CTA button

Do not overload this section with legal wording.

---

### 9. Compliance Section

Topics to include:
- record keeping
- reporting duties
- right to work checks
- genuine vacancy expectations
- HR process readiness
- audit preparation

This section should reinforce that the work is not just about submitting a form.

---

### 10. FAQ Section

## Design Direction
- Use Tailwind CSS via CDN
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Clean, modern, trustworthy business design
- Use premium but restrained visuals
- Strong spacing, readable cards, clear CTA hierarchy
- Use navy, teal, gold, white, and soft neutrals if consistent with the main brand
- Keep the page polished without feeling like a corporate template

Avoid:
- clutter
- gimmicky counters
- red warning-heavy layouts everywhere
- generic stock-legal design

---

## Technical Rules
- Static HTML
- Tailwind via CDN
- Minimal JavaScript
- Semantic HTML and accessible structure
- Sticky header if it improves usability
- Smooth scrolling where helpful
- Clear internal anchors and CTA flow

Optional enhancements:
- document checklist download section
- reveal animations
- sticky CTA on mobile
- trust badges or credibility indicators

---

## Content Guardrails
- Write for employers, not for visa applicants
- Do not promise approval
- Do not oversimplify compliance obligations
- Keep the tone businesslike and reassuring
- Show both application expertise and post-licence awareness
- Use plain English instead of dense legal jargon

---

If an AI is asked to build from this brief, it should:
- produce complete ready-to-run HTML
- avoid filler TODO text
- keep the structure conversion-focused
- make the compliance message clear without sounding intimidating
- use realistic placeholder copy where exact firm details are missing
- return code or files only if the user asks for build output without explanation
